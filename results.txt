2. THE "HIDDEN LOSS" CRISIS (CRITICAL FINDING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Profitable Line Items        : 971 items (64.7%) -> Generated +₹75,042.00 profit
- Loss-Making Line Items       : 529 items (35.3%) -> Destroyed -₹38,079.00 profit
- Impact: 35.3% of all sold items were unprofitable. If loss-making items were
  eliminated or repriced to breakeven, total store profit would DOUBLE from
  ₹36.9K to ₹75.0K without acquiring a single additional customer.

TOP 5 STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS FOR STORE LEADERSHIP:
1. Fix Product Margin Floors: Eliminate or cap discounts on Electronic Games,
   Furnishings, Kurti, and Skirts to stop the ₹38K margin bleed.
2. Incentive Prepaid over COD: Offer a 2-3% checkout incentive for UPI/Cards to
   reduce the 35.4% COD exposure and mitigate delivery cancellation rates.
3. Reprice Regional Logistics: Implement zoned shipping surcharges for Rajasthan
   and Andhra Pradesh to turn those territories profitable.
4. Retention Loyalty Funnels: Engage the 68.2% single-purchase customers with
   automated 30-day post-delivery email/SMS replenishment campaigns.
5. Upgrade Reporting: Replace the misleading 121K AOV visual with accurate DAX
   measures (AOV = ₹875.54) and prioritize Net Margin % alongside gross revenue.
